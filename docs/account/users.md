# Users

## Your Profile

Your account is identified by your email address. You can view your profile and change your password from the dashboard settings.

## Invitation Flow

1. An organization admin sends you an invitation email
2. Click the link in the email to activate your account
3. Set a password and complete registration
4. You are now a member of the organization

## Manage Members (Admin)

Go to **Account → Users** to see all members of the current organization.

Actions available to admins:
- **Change role** — Promote or demote a member's role
- **Remove member** — Remove the user from the organization (their account is not deleted)
- **Invite** — Send a new invitation

## Superadmin

Superadmins have access to the administration panel:
- Manage all organizations
- Manage regions and availability zones
- View all hypervisors and migrations
- Manage alarms

Superadmin status is set at the platform level and cannot be self-assigned.
